Why this matters on the road

Most delay in trauma cases isn't the ambulance ride — it's the 10–15 minutes before anyone even knows where the patient is. SwasthyaSetu removes that gap.

Works with a weak signal

The SOS payload is tiny (location + severity code) so it can go out even on 2G, and retries automatically until it's acknowledged.

Bystanders can raise it too

You don't need to be the patient — anyone at the scene can send the SOS and stay to guide the ambulance in.
