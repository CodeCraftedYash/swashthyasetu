Module 04

QR Hospital Network

Every hospital counter becomes a QR code. Scan it on arrival and you're in the digital queue instantly — no standing in line just to get a token number.

How the scan-in flow works

  • 1
    Scan at the entrance

    One QR code per hospital, printed at the gate and every department desk.

  • 2
    Pick a reason for visit

    OPD consultation, emergency, pharmacy pickup, or report collection — routes you to the right queue.

  • 3
    Get a token instantly

    Shows your number, people ahead of you, and an estimated call time — updates live.

  • 4
    Walk in when notified

    A push alert fires when you're 2 tokens away, so you can wait anywhere — not just the bench.
